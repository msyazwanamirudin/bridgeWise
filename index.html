How the 1-Year Top-Up actually works

Three stages, start to finish — from your Malaysian diploma to a UK-conferred Bachelor's degree.

1

Your Malaysian Diploma

Complete or hold a recognised Diploma in a matching field. Bridge Wise checks your transcript against Sunderland's entry rules before you apply.

2

1 Year at Sunderland, UK

Your diploma is credited toward the final year of the Bachelor's. You study on campus in the UK for one academic year, not three.

3

UK Bachelor's Degree

Graduate with a full Honours degree from a Guardian 2026 Top 30 UK university — the same qualification as a 3-year student.
