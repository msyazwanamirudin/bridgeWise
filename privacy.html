Privacy Policy

How BridgeWise collects, uses, and protects your personal data. Last updated: September 2026.

What we collect

When you use the Apply form, Contact form, or message us on WhatsApp, BridgeWise may collect: your full name, email address, phone number, current institution and diploma details, the program you'd like to apply for, a referral code (if applicable), and a copy of your passport photo page. We only collect what's needed to assess your eligibility and process your application.

How it's used

Your data is used to review your application, confirm eligibility for the 1-Year Top-Up programme, communicate with you about your application status, and — where you proceed with an application — to share the relevant details with the University of Sunderland for admissions purposes. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for purposes beyond what's needed to process your enquiry or application.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are delivered by email via a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit) to BridgeWise's inbox. Passport copies submitted through the Apply form are transmitted the same way. If you're uncomfortable submitting a document by form, you're welcome to send it directly over WhatsApp or email instead — message us and we'll arrange it.

Your rights

Under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), you can ask BridgeWise what personal data we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion of your data where we're not legally required to keep it (for example, once an application has concluded and any retention period has passed). To make a request, email bridgewise.education@gmail.com with your name and the request — we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Cookies & analytics

This site may use Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use it and to measure the effectiveness of our outreach. These tools may set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser. They don't collect your name, email, or application details directly — only general usage patterns (pages visited, general location, device type).

Third parties

Where you submit an application, your data is shared with the University of Sunderland as the receiving institution. We don't share your data with any other third party for marketing purposes.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, message us on WhatsApp or email bridgewise.education@gmail.com.
