The 1-Year Top-Up, track by track

Every programme below runs at the University of Sunderland, a Guardian 2026 Top 30 UK university, and credits your Malaysian diploma toward the final year of a full Honours degree.

£6,000 automatic scholarship

Applied at offer stage — there's no separate scholarship essay, interview, or ranking to compete against.

  • Who qualifies

    Any student who receives an offer for a 1-Year Top-Up programme through Bridge Wise and meets the minimum diploma CGPA for their track.

  • How it's applied

    Deducted directly from your total tuition fee across the programme — it reduces what you owe, it isn't paid out as cash.

  • IELTS waiver

    Available if your diploma was taught and examined in English, subject to the university's document review. Bridge Wise checks this for you before you apply.

  • Instalment plans

    Tuition (after the scholarship is deducted) can be split across the academic year instead of paid as one upfront sum. Bridge Wise's counsellor will walk you through the exact schedule for your track.

Free flight, Sept & Oct intake only

Students confirmed for the September or October 2026 intake receive one economy one-way flight to the UK, arranged by Bridge Wise. This benefit is tied to the intake month, not the track — every eligible programme below qualifies.

Guardian 2026 Top 30

The University of Sunderland is ranked in the Guardian University Guide 2026's Top 30 UK universities — the same national ranking your degree certificate is measured against.

Track details

Entry notes specific to each field of study.

Hospitality Management

Best suited to diploma holders from hotel management, culinary arts, or tourism programmes. Prior internship or practicum hours from your diploma are typically accepted toward the UK programme's placement requirement — bring your logbook to your application review.

Apply for Hospitality

Business Management

Open to Business Studies, Marketing, Accounting, and related diploma holders. This is the most flexible track for mixed or general business diplomas — if you're unsure whether your diploma qualifies, send your transcript over WhatsApp and Bridge Wise will check before you apply.

Apply for Business

Art & Design

For Graphic Design, Fashion Design, or Fine Art diploma holders. A digital portfolio (8–12 pieces) is required alongside your transcript — Bridge Wise can advise on portfolio format before submission.

Apply for Art & Design

Computing

Open to IT, Software Engineering, Computer Science, and Networking diploma holders. Strong programming or systems modules on your transcript are weighed favourably during review.

Apply for Computing

Engineering

For Mechanical, Electrical, Electronic, or Civil Engineering diploma holders. Sub-discipline availability can vary by intake — confirm your exact specialisation with a Bridge Wise counsellor before applying.

Apply for Engineering

Nursing

Reserved for holders of a registered Malaysian Nursing diploma. This track is subject to additional clinical placement and registration checks, so timelines and intake availability can differ from the other five tracks — speak to a counsellor early.

Apply for Nursing
