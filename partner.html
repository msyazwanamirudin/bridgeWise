Refer students. Earn commission. Track every case.

Bridge Wise's sub-agent program is built for education counsellors, tuition centres, and independent recruiters already talking to Malaysian diploma holders about UK options.

How the program works

Three steps from sign-up to payout.

1

Register

Fill in the sub-agent form below. Bridge Wise reviews your details and issues you a personal referral code.

2

Share your code

Your code auto-fills the referral field whenever a student clicks your unique application link — no manual entry, no lost referrals.

3

Earn commission

Once your referred student is confirmed and enrolled, commission is calculated and paid out per Bridge Wise's agent agreement.

Why agents work with Bridge Wise

  • One point of contact

    Every referred student is handled by the same Bridge Wise counsellor from application to enrolment, so you always know who to follow up with.

  • Referral tracking built in

    Your code is captured the moment a student opens your link, before they even start the application form.

  • Real scholarship, real conversions

    The £6,000 automatic scholarship and IELTS waiver make it easier to close students who are on the fence about cost.

Commission structure

Commission is paid per successfully enrolled student and confirmed once the student's first tuition instalment clears with the university. Exact rates depend on track and intake volume.

Registered agents receive the full rate card and payout schedule directly — ask for it during onboarding or via the payout enquiry form below.

Commission terms are confirmed in writing in your individual agent agreement and may vary by track, intake, and enrolment volume.
